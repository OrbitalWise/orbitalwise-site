Business automation

What can be automated in a small business?

Almost any job that is repetitive, rule-based, and currently done by a person copying information between places. In most established businesses that means the same short list: answering and booking calls, chasing follow-ups, sending and chasing invoices, counting and ordering stock, scheduling and dispatching work, and the admin glue between all of it. Orbitalwise is a Memphis-based Day AI partner that builds these back and front office systems for businesses with 50 or fewer employees that are wasting time and money on work a system should be doing.

The front office: where jobs are won and lost

  • Missed calls answered, jobs booked. Every call gets answered, including after hours, and the good ones land on the calendar instead of in a voicemail box nobody checks.
  • Follow-ups that never slip. Estimates, quotes, and inquiries get followed up on a set rhythm until the customer answers. No lead dies because a busy week ate it.
  • Guests, clients, and customers kept warm. Reminders, rebooking nudges, and review requests go out on time, drafted from your records and approved by your people.

The back office: where the hours go

  • Orders and inventory. Counts, par levels, and supplier orders tracked and drafted by the system, with deliveries checked against what was actually ordered.
  • Invoices and payment chasing. Invoices sent on time and followed up with a polite, persistent rhythm until they are paid.
  • Dispatch and scheduling. Crews, routes, and appointments coordinated with confirmations and reminders handled automatically.
  • Admin and CRM upkeep. Records updated from calls, emails, and meetings as they happen, so the system your team looks at is the truth, not last month's version of it.

How it works

Orbitalwise builds on Day AI. First we learn how the work actually happens in your business. Then we build your Business Brain: one living record of your customers, vendors, playbooks, and numbers. Nothing you already run gets ripped out; the system connects the manual work between your tools.

Frequently asked questions

What can be automated in a small business?

Any repetitive, rule-based job: answering and booking calls, lead and estimate follow-ups, invoice sending and chasing, inventory counts and reordering, dispatch and scheduling, appointment reminders, review requests, and CRM data entry.

How much does business automation cost?

It depends on how many jobs the system takes over. Orbitalwise engagements are scoped after a short discovery: we map where your team's hours go, price the build, and the platform runs on a per-agent monthly cost plus a flat monthly fee for the Business Brain. Book a 10-minute call and we will tell you plainly whether the math works for your business.

Do I have to replace my current software?

No. The system is built to work alongside the tools you already run and automate the manual work between them.

Will a robot be talking to my customers?

Only where you want it to, and always inside rules you approve. Agents propose and people approve, especially while the system is earning trust. Most clients start with drafting and tracking, and add customer-facing jobs once they have seen the work.

How long does it take to set up?

Discovery takes days. A Business Brain build is typically a few weeks, and the first one or two jobs go live inside that window so the team is using the system early, not at the end.
